# Home Assistant media player, announcements and Frigate picture-in-picture

Status (2026-10-21): **Home Assistant can fire announcement and camera events, and the Android development app can display them when Settings → TV announcements & cameras is ON.** Layout discovery is unchanged. The events are `kellies_voice_announcement` and `kellies_voice_camera_popup`, sent by the actions `kellies_voice.announce` and `kellies_voice.show_camera`. Install and test steps are in [the integration README](../custom_components/kellies_voice/README.md). This does not expose a `media_player`, play TTS, or stream live Frigate video. Camera alerts show a single authenticated Home Assistant snapshot.

## Intent

Make the onn 4K Pro a **first-class Home Assistant-controlled TV voice/media endpoint**. It remains a normal Google TV box and can accept Home Assistant automation-driven announcements or temporary camera previews without launching a permanent kiosk or dashboard.

The TV app should accept proactive commands over an authenticated, maintained connection even if the voice overlay is currently idle. For example:

- Washer cycle finishes -> one quiet chime and optionally TTS: "The washer is done."
- Frigate detects a **person in an allowed front-door zone** -> "Someone is at the front door" plus a temporary picture-in-picture live doorbell feed.
- Speak "show driveway" -> open the live driveway feed; "full screen" expands it; Back closes it.
- Speak "show garage dashboard" -> open allowlisted Home Assistant view, only on request.
- HA script / automation -> play TTS, an announcement chime, or (future) an allowed audio media URL through the TV speakers.

## Suggested Home Assistant entities

Create a small custom HA integration with a configuration flow, registered TV device and at least:

- `media_player.living_room_tv_voice` (actual slug configurable): available/unavailable state, volume_level, volume_set, mute/unmute if controllable, play_media, supported media content types, and TTS support through `tts.speak` or HA media source. Do **not** claim capabilities the Android app has not implemented.
- `assist_satellite.living_room_tv` (later): exposes native HA voice assistant state, announcement and conversation behavior when implementation supports it. It must not be a prerequisite for media-player MVP.
- Config entities such as `switch.living_room_tv_announcements`, `switch.living_room_tv_camera_popups`, volume, quiet-hours controls, timeout, and camera allowlist, where practical.

A generic Android TV media_player exposed by another integration is not equivalent to a dedicated announcement/overlay service. Prefer our own integration to target our app directly. Avoid entity collisions with other Android TV / Google Cast media players.

## Communication architecture

- **HA custom integration ↔ native TV app**, over a persistent authenticated channel on the LAN, e.g. WebSocket, with ping/heartbeat, reconnect/backoff, command IDs, acknowledgements, capability negotiation and device registration. Which end hosts the socket is a technical decision; avoid making the TV externally reachable.
- Commands: `announce`, `play_media`, `show_camera`, `hide_camera`, `show_dashboard`, `dismiss_overlay`, `set_volume`, `stop`, `get_status`.
- Important to separate **transient announcements** from media playback. In a first pass, implement announcement TTS and camera popups rather than claiming full music streaming, arbitrary casting or unsupported volume controls.
- Support TTS generated by HA, fetched securely by the client as a short-lived media URL or streamed via authenticated proxy. No hard-coded bearer tokens/RTSP passwords in URL parameters stored in the public repo.
- Device authorization/pairing, TLS/certificate verification where appropriate, revocable secret in Android Keystore, and only preapproved dashboard/camera paths.
- Notification security: allow only trusted HA commands; do not accept arbitrary speech-supplied HTTP URLs.
- Handle offline TVs and standby cleanly: entity marks unavailable, do not queue stale doorbell video to auto-play hours later.

## Audio experience

- Default: a short chime and speech, configurable on/off independently.
- Request transient audio focus and prefer `AUDIOFOCUS_GAIN_TRANSIENT_MAY_DUCK` when appropriate; handle apps that don't support ducking and restore volume/focus reliably after the announcement.
- Do not assume arbitrary DRM streaming apps allow mixing or controlled ducking. Test Netflix, Stremio, Nuvio, YouTube, and the launcher on the actual device.
- Respect TV master volume, separate announcement volume control where feasible, DND/silent/quiet hours, "do not interrupt movies" option, and notifications off entirely.
- Priority/queue: low-priority washer status can wait until idle; higher-priority doorbell can chime immediately if enabled. Avoid multiple overlapping audio requests and duplicate speech.
- Optional caption bubble for noisy rooms or muted playback; display duration configurable and dismiss with Back.
- Never automatically resume paused third-party media via blind remote key presses; rely on OS audio focus or restore only the playback state we own.

## Frigate camera PiP / overlay behavior

Two related but distinct Android features must be tested:

1. **Transient app overlay window** (e.g. `TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY`) can draw a customizable corner camera mini-window over the currently active TV app if permission/OEM rules allow. On DRM-secured media surfaces it may not appear. It needs close/full-screen focus and D-pad interaction design.
2. **Android picture-in-picture activity** is system-managed and may have limitations on position, concurrent videos, codecs and OEM TV behavior. It may be a fallback or preferred mode depending on real-device tests, but should not be assumed identical to overlay.

### Trigger rules for doorbell notifications

- Consume **Frigate events** via Home Assistant automation, filtering by a person recognized in a configured zone (e.g. front-door approach) rather than every motion.
- Configurable cooldown (e.g. 60–120 sec), dedupe by Frigate event ID, and quiet-hours privacy rules.
- Prefer low-latency **go2rtc/WebRTC**, fallback HLS/MP4 or HA camera streaming if codecs or Android TV APIs require; authenticate each stream, avoid opening unrestricted go2rtc admin UI.
- Small corner PiP appears for e.g. 15–30 sec (configurable); full screen on OK or by voice; Back closes, original TV app stays visible wherever firmware permits.
- Only show automatically for allowed cameras/triggers. Explicit voice command can request any *authorized* configured camera by friendly name.
- When TV is asleep/off, respect no-wake policy by default. Never power on the TV for a routine notice without opt-in.
- Later optional two-way talk is separate, not promised for MVP.

## Acceptance tests

1. With a streaming app playing, trigger HA automation `tts.speak`/announcement to our dedicated media_player; hear "Washer is done" once, with correct volume management and playback recovery.
2. Confirm media_player becomes unavailable when the box is disconnected and available when reconnecting.
3. Trigger an allowed Frigate person event; see a small live doorbell feed in the corner and optional spoken announcement, with dedupe/cooldown.
4. Select PiP and grow to full screen; Back returns to prior content without returning to our own launcher.
5. Ask by voice "show driveway camera" and see a live feed.
6. Disable announcement/camera popups, or set quiet hours: the TV must respect user settings.
7. Confirm unknown media URLs/camera names are rejected, no secret or token leaks into logs/public source.

## Revised priority

0. **Completed for foreground mode:** real onn mic/D-pad probe, HA WebSocket token auth and first successful speech-to-text question. **Not yet completed:** audio focus, overlay permissions and shortcut tests.
1. Verify reliable TTS playback, answer-first UI, microphone lifecycle and signed APK update; **then** build a minimal transient Assist overlay.
2. **Dedicated HA media player and announcement pipeline**, including washer-finished use case.
3. **Frigate event-driven picture-in-picture camera popups**, with spoken doorbell notices and cooldown.
4. Rich chat panels, timer and media previews, on-demand dashboards and multi-camera view.
5. Optional wake word and advanced Assist Satellite/media features.

All **media player, automation announcement and Frigate PiP** functionality described here is a design target, **not shipped**. Camera overlay support over streaming/DRM content remains untested. The Google voice button has been observed opening Google Assistant; the onn box microphone already works for foreground Assist STT, but Bluetooth remote mic access is unverified.
